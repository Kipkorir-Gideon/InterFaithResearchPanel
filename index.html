The Interfaith Research Panel for a Living Planet (IRePLiP)

The Interfaith Research Panel for a Living Planet (IRePLiP) is panel of experts spearheading evidence based decision making and research for a better living planet with key activities as follows;

  • Provide long-term and consistent data and information important for solving world challenges.
  • Providing evidence/scientific based information for key decision making.
  • Liaising with organizations, researchers, operational and end-user bodies in order to determine and maintain the requirements for data to monitor, understand and predict contribution to solving global challenges.
  • Improve communication and foster inter-disciplinary and comparative approaches on research among various stakeholders.
  • A platform to disseminate research findings and projects in order to create evidence based decisions.

IRePliP shall:

  • Periodically convene the IRePliP congress as well as regional symposia on scientific, religious, political and practical issues in order to establish contacts among its members.
  • Encourage an open collaboration for the development of research programs and other collaborative partnership globally.
  • Endorse applications to make research funds or facilities available to IRePLiP members and other selected group.
  • Promote relations with associations and institutions that share the goals of the IRePLiP.

IRePlip aims to constitute an inclusive network, to act as an open platform, and to provide a framework to foster evidence based decision making through research, effective dissemination of information, exchange and cooperation concerning important issues.

Vision and Mission

Vision

Let it be for a Living Planet.

Mission

To make lives better through evidence based decision/research and sharing of knowledge.

Values

Innovation

Emerging data, new tools and a new generation evidence based decision making.

Respect

Towards various decision makers for a sustainable future.

Enrichment

Evidence based decision making through research, digital access and collaboration.
